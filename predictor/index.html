How the model works

Each team gets an attack and defence rating from its goals scored and conceded per game this season (home and away splits, shrunk toward the league average when the sample is small). Expected goals = league-average home/away goals × home attack × away defence (and vice versa), nudged up to ±8% by points-per-game over the last 5 matches. Champions League ratings also blend in each club's domestic-league ratings when that league is covered. Goals are modelled as independent Poisson variables (with a small Dixon–Coles low-score correction) to get win/draw/loss, scoreline, over/under 2.5 and both-teams-to-score probabilities. "Fair odds" are simply 1 ÷ probability. Data: ESPN public scoreboard & standings feeds.

Predictions are statistical estimates, not guarantees. This site shows analysis only and does not take or link to bets.
